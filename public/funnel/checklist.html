The checklist

10 steps, in order.

  1. Know your comfortable monthly numberDecide what you want to pay each month before anyone tells you what you "qualify" for.
  2. Check your credit reportsPull all three for free, dispute any errors, and don't open new accounts while you shop.
  3. Save for more than the down paymentClosing costs, moving, and a cushion for the first repairs all land in the same few months.
  4. Gather your documentsTwo years of tax returns, two months of bank statements, recent pay stubs and a photo ID.
  5. Get pre-approved, not just pre-qualifiedIn Orange County, sellers expect a real pre-approval letter with your offer.
  6. Learn the local extrasMello-Roos, HOA dues and special assessments change the monthly picture in many OC neighborhoods.
  7. Ask about first-time buyer programsDown-payment assistance and state programs exist; eligibility varies, so ask early.
  8. Pick your agent and loan officer as a teamThey should talk to each other — in the language you prefer.
  9. Budget time for inspectionsGeneral, termite and sewer on older homes; roof and drainage near the coast.
  10. Plan your move-in monthProperty taxes, insurance and utilities start right away — set the dates before you close.
