FAQ

Good questions to ask.

How much do I need for a down payment?

It depends on the loan program. Some programs allow low down payments for qualified buyers. We'll compare your options on a short call.

Does asking for a call affect my credit?

No. Requesting a call-back doesn't pull your credit. If you decide to apply later, I'll tell you before anything is checked.

I'm self-employed. Can I still qualify?

Often, yes. There are programs designed for business owners and non-traditional income. Bring your questions — we'll sort out what's realistic.

How long does it take to close?

Many purchases close in about 30 days once a complete file is in, but every file is different. You'll get a timeline on day one.

Can we talk in Vietnamese?

Yes — every call, email and document walkthrough can be in Vietnamese or English, whichever you prefer.
